ABOUT SCREENLENS

Local video scene intelligence for NVIDIA DGX Spark and Apple Silicon.

What it does

Ingests screen recordings, extracts keyframes, captions them with a vision-language model, embeds them with OpenCLIP and answers natural-language questions from ChromaDB. A second pipeline rebuilds the visible files and documents; a third transcribes them character-for-character and stitches scroll overlap in text space.

Model roles

Two roles against one OpenAI-compatible endpoint. The vision model reads frames (captions, OCR) and must be vision-capable. The text model does the reasoning (summaries, reconstruction plan/QA, transcript cleanup) and never sees an image. On DGX Spark both resolve to the single served vLLM checkpoint.

Stack
